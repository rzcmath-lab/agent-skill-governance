# Evidence Policy

A reusable rule needs more than a memorable incident.

## Evidence levels

1. **Observation** — one report or trace; useful for investigation, not enough for a universal rule.
2. **Corroboration** — the same failure is independently reproduced or supported by another source.
3. **Validated lesson** — a bounded intervention changes the expected outcome under stated conditions.

Only levels 2–3 normally justify admission. Level 1 may justify a conditional diagnostic note when the uncertainty is explicit.

Record provenance at the level needed for review, while removing identities, private paths, payloads, and organization-specific details.
